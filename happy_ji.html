A Message for You 💌

Happy Birthday meri Bubu ❤️

Happy Birthday meri Bubu ❤️ Aaj ka din calendar par baaki duniya ke liye bas ek normal tareekh ho sakti hai, lekin mere liye ye saal ka sabse khoobsurat aur khaas din hai. Kyunki aaj hi ke din is duniya ko woh noor mila tha, jisne aage chal kar meri poori duniya hi roshan kar di.

Kabhi kabhi main akele baith kar sochta hoon ki waqt kitni khamoshi se sab kuch kitna khoobsurat bana deta hai, haina? Hum kahan bas ek dusre ko thoda bahut jaante the, ek normal si dosti thi hamari. Lekin waqt ke sath pata hi nahi chala ki kab aap meri life ka sabse zaroori hissa ban gayi. Kab aapki ek smile meri poore din ki thakan mitaane lagi, aur kab bina aapse baat kiye mera din adhura sa lagne laga.

Aap sirf meri 'best friend' ya 'special person' nahi hain, aap mere liye ek sukoon hain. Jab aas-paas sab kuch uljha hua aur chaotic lagta hai, tab aapki baatein, aapki aawaz, aur aapka mere sath hona mujhe ek alag si shanti deta hai. Aapne mujhe sikhaya hai ki bina kuch kahe kisi ko poori tarah se samajhna aur use apna man-na kya hota hai.

Main hamesha kehta hoon aur aaj phir kahunga—aap shayad khud nahi jaanti ki aap kitni pyari aur anmol hain. Aapki woh chhoti-chhoti baaton par khush ho jana, aapka har kisi ki care karna, aapki baaton ki masoomiyat aur aapki sabse khoobsurat smile... ye sab milkar aapko itna perfect banate hain ki main bas dekhta reh jaun. Aapki is saadgi ne hi mere dil ko sabse zyada chhua hai.

Aksar log pyaar ko bade-bade shabdon mein dhoondhte hain, lekin maine us pyaar ko aapki fikr mein, aapke gusse mein, aur aapke us chhote se 'take care' mein mehsoos kiya hai. Main shayad duniya ka sabse bada shayar na ban paun jo aapke liye har roz nayi linein likhe, lekin main woh insaan zaroor banna chahta hoon jo aapki aankhon mein kabhi aansoo na aane de. I love you, genuinely, deeply, and unconditionally.

Aaj aapke is special din par, main aapke liye sirf aaj ki khushiyan nahi, balki aane wale poore future ki behisaab kamiyabi maangta hoon. Aapke andar jitna potential hai, aap usse kahin zyada deserve karti hain. Zindagi mein aage badhte hue kabhi bhi khud par doubt mat karna. Aapke sapne bahut bade hain aur mujhe poora yakeen hai ki apni mehnat aur lagan se aap unhe ek din haqeeqat mein zaroor badlengi.

Mujhe pata hai life hamesha asaan nahi hoti. Kuch din aise zaroor aayenge jab aap thaka hua feel karengi, jab lagega ki cheezein waisi nahi ho rahi hain jaisi aap chahti hain. Un dino mein, main chahta hoon ki aap hamesha yaad rakhein ki aap akeli nahi hain. Main hamesha aapke peeche khada rahunga—aapki himmat bankar. Aap girengi toh main uthaunga, aap rukengi toh main aapka hath pakad kar aapke sath chalunga.

Main dua karta hoon ki is naye saal mein aapko woh har khushi mile jiske baare mein aapne akele mein socha hai. Aapki har subah ek nayi umeed ke sath shuru ho aur har raat ek pyare se sukoon ke sath khatam ho. Aapki life mein rone ki wajah ek bhi na ho, aur muskurane ke bahaane hazaar hon.

Aur ek sabse zaroori baat—chahe duniya kitni bhi badal jaye, waqt kitna bhi aage badh jaye, aap kabhi mat badalna. Apne andar ki us masoom si, pure-hearted aur pyari si Bubu ko hamesha zinda rakhna. Duniya ko waise bhi aap jaise genuine logon ki hi sabse zyada zaroorat hai.

Aaj ke din aap kisi bhi cheez ki tension mat lijiye, bas apni is special day ko poori tarah se jeeyiye. Aap apne aap mein ek masterpiece hain, aur main khud ko is duniya ka sabse lucky insaan maanta hoon ki life ne mujhe aapko padhne ka, samajhne ka aur is hadd tak chahne ka mauka diya.

Happy Birthday meri sabse pyari, sabse khaas, aur meri eklauti favourite person. May God bless you with everything your beautiful heart desires. Welcome to a brand new, amazing chapter of your life. Keep shining, keep smiling, and always remember—you are loved beyond measure. ❤️

— With all my love, Prince ❤️
